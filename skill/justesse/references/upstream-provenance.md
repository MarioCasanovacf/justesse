# Upstream Provenance

This skill is an original, stack-neutral adaptation. Its rules are written in this repository's own
voice, and absorbed ideas enter only as candidate rules subject to the calibration described in
[profile-calibration.md](profile-calibration.md). Two upstream projects inspired identifiable parts
of it; both are pinned here with their licenses.

## Leonxlnx/taste-skill

- Upstream URL: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill
- Pinned source commit: b17742737e796305d829b3ad39eda3add0d79060
- Relevant upstream source: skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md and its sibling specialist skills
- License: MIT, retained in full at `../LICENSE.upstream`

Inspired the design-judgment framing: brief inference, visual-direction dials, anti-generic
heuristics, redesign discipline, motion restraint, accessibility, and preflight concepts. The
adaptation expands the usable scope to product UI, dashboards, transactional and multi-step flows,
reference-to-code work, visual review, and image concept direction. It preserves the target
project's framework and product contracts, treats motion and image tools as conditional, and does
not inherit mandatory assumptions about React, Next.js, Tailwind, GSAP, dark mode, fonts, remote
tools, punctuation, random aesthetic selection, or a fixed image-per-section rule.

## pbakaus/impeccable

- Upstream URL: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Pinned source commit: c8f476b330395031bc8f7a7aee8d848bc85c81e4
- Relevant upstream source: .agents/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md,
  reference/craft-floor.md, and reference/operate.md
- License: Apache-2.0, retained in full at `../LICENSE.upstream-impeccable`

Inspired three distillations, each rewritten as original stack-neutral prose: the built-result
verification checks and unearned-defaults catalog in
[interface-quality.md](interface-quality.md), the bounded-verification discipline in the core
workflow, and the reading-surface mode in [reading-surfaces.md](reading-surfaces.md). The
adaptation does not inherit impeccable's runtime machinery (setup scripts, hooks, live browser
iteration, agent definitions), its command surface, or its maximalist default posture; in this
skill the brief and the product contract remain governing, and profiles remain declared rather
than bundled.

Keep each source URL, pinned commit, provenance note, and license together when redistributing or
revising this adaptation.
