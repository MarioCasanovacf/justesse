# Style Lenses

Use these as diagnostic lenses when the brief lacks visual language. A lens is not a template,
package, or instruction to copy every associated trope.

## Quiet precision

Calm hierarchy, exact spacing, restrained contrast, durable typography, and minimal motion. Useful
for trust, focus, editorial reading, and high-consequence tasks. Avoid confusing quiet with low
contrast, generic whitespace, or missing affordances.

## Expressive editorial

Strong type relationships, narrative pacing, image-led composition, and controlled asymmetry.
Useful when voice and story carry the experience. Preserve scanability, reading order, mobile
rhythm, and action clarity.

## Technical instrument

Dense but ordered information, precise state language, sharp alignment, and functional color.
Useful for developer tools, operations, analytics, and expert workflows. Avoid decorative terminal
motifs, fake telemetry, and density that obscures exceptions or actions.

## Human material

Warm color, tactile surfaces, candid imagery, softened geometry, and patient motion. Useful for
care, craft, community, and approachable services. Avoid generic beige, invented people, excessive
rounding, or sentiment that weakens clarity.

## Bold graphic

High contrast, decisive scale, strong geometry, and sparse high-impact composition. Useful for
campaigns, culture, launches, and portfolios. Protect accessibility, content hierarchy, and
responsive behavior; boldness is not random collision.

## Selecting a lens

Choose the lens whose mechanics support the audience, job, content, and brand evidence. Combine at
most two with one clearly dominant. Translate the choice into concrete rules for type, grid, color,
surface, imagery, and motion. Reject any rule that conflicts with an existing product contract.

## Amplifying within a lens

When a surface already has its visual world and one part reads flat, the reflex answer, adding
effects, is the opposite of bold; reject it first. A section usually reads flat because it quietly
opts out of the system's own strongest moves: the display type at full strength, the structural
devices that carry meaning, the signature motif, the density and pacing its neighbors already use.
The reliable amplification brings the target up to the expressive level the rest of the page
already reaches, in the system's own vocabulary, without adding a color, font, radius, or primitive
the surface does not already own.

- Scope is sovereign: touch only the named target and leave its neighbors, claims, and behaviors
  unchanged. If the existing system genuinely cannot express the direction, name the exact addition
  and the job it would do, and ask instead of expanding the system on your own authority.
- Commit, then quiet. Make one decisive move completely and calm everything around it so the move
  is legible. If every element got louder, the section got flatter.
- Apply the skeleton test: strip the copy and study the bare structure. If the section only says
  what it is once the words return, the boldness lives in the text size, not the design.

The same discipline runs in reverse for quieting an overloud surface: reduce the competing moves to
one, in the system's own vocabulary, rather than desaturating everything into genericness.
